## 1. What this annex is — and is not

This annex lists **the two GSI post-disaster (PDLS) Wayanad records that exist in the project
inventory**. It is an extract for the agency's reference, so that the request is unambiguous about
what is already held.

It is **not** a list of July 2024 landslide events. It does not add, estimate, infer or imply any
further record, and it contains no coordinate derived from narrative prose or from a map.

Both records come from GSI's *Post-Disaster Studies of Landslides in Kerala* volumes. Both are point
records of a 42-point geo-parametric datasheet, not footprints or extents.

## 3. Project verification status (our assessment, not GSI's)

| Record | Status in the inventory | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `KER/WYD/58A03/2024/01` | `SOURCE_CONFIRMED` | Primary GSI field investigation; coordinate retained exactly as printed |
| `KER/WYD/58A03/2025/01` | `SOURCE_CONFIRMED` | Primary GSI field investigation; declared history places the event in June 2024, reported 21.05.2025 |

For the 2024 event, the inventory's event group `EV-2024-MUNDAKKAI` also contains one independent
peer-reviewed record (Sreekumar et al. 2025, Table 1: 11.466944 N, 76.135833 E). Measured separation
in EPSG:32643 is **495 m**, so the two are treated as one event group and the `CORROBORATED` status
sits on that independent record — **not** on the GSI datasheet. For context, the GSI point lies
1.98 km from Mundakkai village and 4.91 km from Chooralmala settlement, consistent with a source-area
location rather than a runout centroid.

Record 2 has no independent corroboration recorded. It is a 90 m² road-cut rotational slide, not a
2025 event.

## 4. Place names in the 2024 report prose

The 2024 volume's first information report (same PDF, p. 95) names **Chooralmala, Mundakkai,
Attamalai and Vellarimala** in narrative prose describing the disaster; the report prints
"Vellaramala" once as a variant spelling.

Only the **Mundakkai–Chooralmala** site has a datasheet in the volume. The other three names are
**place names mentioned in report prose only**. They are not records in this annex, they are not
counted as events anywhere in the project, and no coordinate has been derived from that narrative.
They are listed here solely so the scope of the accompanying questions is clear.

## 6. Internal note — **do not enclose**

- The extract column `coord_precision_deg` stores `2.778e-06` (0.01″ ≈ 0.31 m) for **both** rows,
  because `scripts/p2_parse_gsi_datasheets.py` hard-codes `round(0.01 / 3600, 9)` at L85 and L131.
  For the 2024 record the printed coordinate has **whole seconds**, so the stored value overstates
  the precision the source supports by ~100×. This annex states the precision **as printed** (whole
  second ≈ 31 m), matching `docs/INVENTORY_VERIFICATION_REPORT.md` §4 and the covering letter.
  Correcting the CSV is a Phase 2 product change and a user decision; it was **not** made in this run.
- The 2024 datasheet starts on p. 95 and its table continues onto p. 96 (page marker at text-extract
  line 3378). The letter's "p. 95" refers to the datasheet's first page and is consistent.
- The 2024 datasheet has no field 40/41, which is why `date_of_reporting` is empty for that row.
