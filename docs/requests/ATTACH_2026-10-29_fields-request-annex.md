If site-level records for the July 2024 Wayanad landslides are available, the fields below are the
ones that make them usable for landslide **susceptibility** research — that is, for mapping *where*
terrain conditions favour failure. No field is mandatory. A partial answer, or "not recorded", is
useful; nothing needs to be estimated or completed on our behalf.

| # | Field | Why it matters | Acceptable form |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record / site identifier | Lets the record be cited as the agency's own, rather than under an identifier we invent | Any printed ID or serial number |
| 2 | Event date (and time if recorded) | Separates the 30 July 2024 event from the June 2024 event and from later failures | As printed |
| 3 | Coordinates **as printed**, with any decimal conversion | Preserves the source's own precision and prevents silent rounding | Printed text plus numbers |
| 4 | Coordinate reference system | Required before any distance, slope or area calculation; our analysis is done in EPSG:32643 | EPSG code, datum, or "not stated" |
| 5 | Stated positional precision or accuracy | Determines whether a point can be used spatially at all | Metres, arc-seconds, or the printed coordinate format |
| 6 | Slide type, material, mechanism, rate of movement | Separates debris flows from shallow translational and rotational slides | As printed |
| 7 | Dimensions, area, volume, runout | Context for interpreting the point; often the only extent information available | As printed, including blank/"--" |
| 8 | Source document, page and figure | Provenance for every row and for any later citation | Citation or page reference |
| 9 | Access conditions and terms of use | Determines what may be published or redistributed | Licence, written condition, or "on request" |
| 10 | Required citation form | So credit is given exactly as the agency wishes | Text |

## Notes

- **Formats.** A CSV, spreadsheet, shapefile, GeoPackage or scanned datasheet are all acceptable. A
  scan or photograph of a printed 42-point datasheet is sufficient if no digital table exists.
- **Partial answers are fine.** If only some sites or some fields are available, that is still useful;
  please indicate what is withheld rather than leaving it ambiguous.
- **No personal data is requested.** Nothing about individuals, landowners, casualties by name, or
  internal correspondence is sought.
- **Restricted fields.** If a field or site is restricted, stating that it is restricted — without
  the value — is a complete answer for our records.
- **What we will do with it.** Record it with its provenance, cite the agency, and observe whatever
  conditions are attached. It would not be redistributed without permission.
