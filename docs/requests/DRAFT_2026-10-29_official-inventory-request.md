[DATE]

To
The Director General
Geological Survey of India
Kolkata – 700016

**Subject: Request for information on official geospatial landslide records and post-disaster
datasheets for the July 2024 landslides in Wayanad district, Kerala**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am [NAME], [DESIGNATION] at [INSTITUTION / DEPARTMENT], [CITY]. My work concerns landslide
**susceptibility** mapping for Wayanad district — that is, estimating *where* terrain and
environmental conditions favour failure. It does not forecast, warn about, or date any future
landslide, and nothing in this request is intended for that purpose. I am writing to ask whether
additional official records from your 2024 post-disaster work in Wayanad are available, and, if so,
how they may be obtained and used.

**What my inventory currently contains.** I maintain a small research inventory of Wayanad landslide
source records covering 2000–2026, built only from published sources. From GSI's *Post-Disaster
Studies of Landslides in Kerala* volumes it currently contains exactly two Wayanad datasheet
records:

- the 2024 volume's Mundakkai–Chooralmala datasheet (record `KER/WYD/58A03/2024/01`, PDF p. 95),
  printed as 11°27′46″N / 76°08′03″E — 11.462778 N, 76.134167 E — whole-second DMS, about 31 m
  ground resolution;
- the 2025 volume's Meenakshi Bridge datasheet (record `KER/WYD/58A03/2025/01`, PDF p. 80), whose
  declared history places the failure in June 2024.

I checked the 2024 coordinate against an independent peer-reviewed compilation (Sreekumar et al.
2025, Table 1) and found the two agree to within 495 m, which is consistent with both locating the
same source area rather than a runout centroid. I record it as a source-area point, not as a
footprint.

**On the four place names.** The 2024 volume's first information report (p. 95 of the same PDF)
names Chooralmala, Mundakkai, Attamalai and Vellarimala in narrative prose describing the event
(the report also prints "Vellaramala" once). I record these strictly as **place names mentioned in
report prose**. They are not, in my inventory, four verified landslide records, and I have not
derived, inferred or interpolated any coordinate from that narrative. I mention them only so that
the scope of my question is clear.

**What I am asking.** I would be grateful to know:

1. Whether any post-disaster datasheets, site record sheets or field sheets for the 30 July 2024
   Wayanad event are held **beyond** the Mundakkai–Chooralmala datasheet at p. 95 — and if so, for
   which sites, and whether they are available for research use.
2. Whether coordinate tables, point files, or GIS layers (shapefile, GeoPackage, CSV or similar)
   associated with the 2024 and 2025 Kerala volumes are available — specifically any Wayanad subset.
3. Whether a mapped extent, runout or deposition footprint polygon for the 2024 event exists in
   vector form.
4. If a machine-readable subset exists: what coordinate reference system it is stored in (for
   example EPSG:4326 or EPSG:32643), and what positional precision or accuracy the coordinates
   carry as published.
5. Which attribute fields each record carries — for example a site or record identifier, event date
   and time, coordinates as printed, slide type and mechanism, dimensions, volume, runout, and the
   geomorphology / lithology / land-use / trigger fields used in the datasheet series.
6. The procedure for obtaining "soft copy of the report and digital data ... with the permission of
   the Director General" as printed in the GSI volumes: the form or written request required, the
   designated officer, any fee, and the expected turnaround.
7. The access conditions and terms of use that would apply — licence, citation requirement,
   redistribution restrictions, and whether derived non-identifying coordinates may be published in
   academic work with citation.
8. If any of this material is exempt, restricted or not held, whether you could indicate which
   category applies, so that my records state the access status accurately rather than guessing.
9. Whether there is a preferred nodal officer or office for data requests of this kind, or another
   office to which this request should be redirected.

**What I am not asking for.** I am not requesting personal data about individuals, internal
deliberations, or any material that is not cleared for release. I will not seek the same material
through any other channel if you indicate that it is restricted. If the answer is that no further
records are available, I will record that as the answer and will not treat it as evidence that no
such records exist.

I would be glad to provide any clarification, a short project note, or a formal undertaking on data
handling and citation. I can be reached at [EMAIL] or [PHONE].

Thank you for your time and for the field work your teams carried out in Wayanad.

Yours faithfully,

[NAME]
[DESIGNATION], [INSTITUTION / DEPARTMENT]
[POSTAL ADDRESS]
[EMAIL] · [PHONE]
[ORCID or institutional profile — optional]

## 4. Fields requested (attach as a one-page annex if useful)

| # | Field | Why it matters | Acceptable form |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record / site identifier | Lets us cite the agency's own record rather than inventing an ID | Any printed ID |
| 2 | Event date (and time if recorded) | Distinguishes the 30 July 2024 event from June 2024 and later events | As printed |
| 3 | Coordinates **as printed**, plus decimal conversion | Preserves source precision; prevents silent rounding | Text + numbers |
| 4 | Coordinate reference system | Required before any metric analysis (we work in EPSG:32643) | EPSG code or datum |
| 5 | Stated positional precision / accuracy | Determines whether a point can be used at all | Metres or arc-seconds |
| 6 | Slide type, material, mechanism, rate | Needed to separate debris flows from shallow translational slides | As printed |
| 7 | Dimensions, area, volume, runout | Context for interpreting the point | As printed |
| 8 | Source document, page, figure | Provenance for every row | Citation |
| 9 | Access conditions and terms of use | Determines what we may publish | Licence or written condition |
| 10 | Required citation form | So credit is given as the agency wishes | Text |

If a field is not recorded by the agency, "not recorded" is a complete and useful answer — it does
not need to be filled in.

## 5. Wording rules this draft follows (check before sending)

- Says "**whether any … are available / held**", never "please send the additional datasheets".
- Describes Chooralmala, Mundakkai, Attamalai and Vellarimala as **prose mentions**, not records,
  and states explicitly that no coordinate was derived from the prose.
- Names the **one** held Wayanad datasheet from the 2024 volume; makes no claim about how many
  others exist.
- Uses no forecast, warning, prediction or timing language, and does not describe the project as an
  early-warning system.
- Asks for access **conditions** as well as data, so a refusal or restriction is recorded as an
  answer rather than as an absence.
- Contains no claim about unpublished material, and no suggestion that the agency has failed to
  publish something.
